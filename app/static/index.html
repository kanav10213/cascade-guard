Cascade Guard

checking server…

In a chain of AI agents, one agent's mistake gets accepted by the next and builds up until it reaches the final answer. Cascade Guard checks every step, re-runs a failing step, and stops the order if it can't be fixed, so a wrong answer is never delivered silently.
