# Kestrel Precision Tools Pvt Ltd: IT and Security Policy

Sample policy for a made-up company, for trying Cascade Guard.

## Passwords and sign-in
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and are changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
is required for email, the HR portal and the VPN.

## Laptops
Every employee gets a company laptop. Laptops must have disk encryption turned on and must not be left unlocked.
Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

## Data
Customer drawings and price lists are confidential. They may not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or to
public AI chat tools. Use the company's own assistant for questions about company documents.

it-help@kestrel-tools.example, extension 2300, open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days.
