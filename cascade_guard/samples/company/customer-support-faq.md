# Kestrel Precision Tools: Customer Support FAQ

Sample FAQ for a made-up company, for trying Cascade Guard.

## How long is the warranty?
Power tools carry a 2-year warranty from the invoice date, except the angle grinder (KPT-AG-100), which carries
1 year. Hand tools carry 1 year, and the 36-piece socket set has a lifetime warranty.

## Can I return a product?
Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 15 days of delivery for a full refund.
Refunds reach the original payment method within 7 working days.

## How do I get a warranty repair?
Raise a ticket at support.kestrel-tools.example with the invoice number and a photo of the fault. Our service
partner collects the tool within 3 working days in metro cities and within 6 working days elsewhere.

## What are the support hours?
Phone support: 1800-000-0000 (toll-free), Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
