# Kestrel Precision Tools Pvt Ltd: Travel and Expense Policy

Sample policy for a made-up company, for trying Cascade Guard.

## Approval limits
Expenses up to ₹5,000 are approved by the employee's manager. Expenses from ₹5,001 to ₹50,000 need the
department head's approval. Anything above ₹50,000 needs approval from the Director of Finance.

## Claiming expenses
Submit claims within 30 days of the expense, through the expense portal. Claims older than 60 days are not paid.
An original receipt or GST invoice is required for every expense above ₹500. Reimbursements are paid with the
next salary, on the last working day of the month.

## Daily allowance (per diem)
Domestic travel: ₹1,500 per day in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune)
and ₹1,000 per day elsewhere. The allowance covers meals and local travel; no receipts are needed for it.

## Hotels
Hotel limits per night: ₹7,000 in metro cities and ₹4,500 in other cities. Managers and above may book up to
₹9,000 per night in metro cities.

## Travel class
Trains: AC 2-tier for all employees. Flights: economy class, booked at least 14 days ahead where possible.
Cabs: Uber or Ola to and from airports and stations are allowed; keep the e-receipt.

## Not reimbursed
Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, and upgrades to a higher travel class.
