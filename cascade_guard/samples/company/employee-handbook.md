# Kestrel Precision Tools Pvt Ltd: Employee Handbook

This is a sample company handbook made up for trying Cascade Guard. Kestrel Precision Tools is not a real company.

## 1. Working hours
The office is open Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, with a one-hour lunch break. The second and fourth
Saturdays of each month are working days for the factory team only, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week with their manager's approval. Work from home is not available
during the first 90 days of employment.

## 2. Leave policy
Every permanent employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month.
Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 March.

Sick leave: 8 days per year. A doctor's certificate is needed for more than 2 sick days in a row.

Casual leave: 6 days per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks with full pay. Paternity leave is 10 working days with full pay, to be taken within
3 months of the child's birth.

Leave requests go through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance, except sick leave.

## 3. Probation and notice period
New employees are on probation for 6 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days.
After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for staff and 90 days for managers and above.

## 4. Holidays for 2026
The company observes 12 public holidays in 2026, including Republic Day (26 January), Holi (4 March),
Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October) and Diwali (8 November and 9 November).
The full list is on the HR portal.
