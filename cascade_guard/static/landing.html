Why "checked" matters

An ordinary AI chatbot can state a wrong price or invent a policy, and your customer will hold you to it. This one checks its own answer against your document first.

An ordinary AI chatbot can say

"What's the warranty on the angle grinder?"

"All our power tools come with a 2-year warranty."

Wrong: the document says 1 year for that product.

With checking, the customer sees

"What's the warranty on the angle grinder?"

"The angle grinder (KPT-AG-100) carries a 1-year warranty."

✓ Checked against: customer-support-faq

How it checks: every amount, number, date and quotation in the answer must appear in the passage it came from. A wrong one is sent back to be corrected, and anything that still can't be confirmed is removed or marked. The checks reduce mistakes a lot; no AI system is perfect, so answers that could not be fully confirmed are shown with a warning.

Questions

What can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), text, Markdown, CSV and HTML files. Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) need to be converted to text first. Save Excel sheets as CSV.

Is my data private?

Your documents sit in your own workspace, which other customers cannot see. They are not used to train AI models. You can delete them at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens when the documents don't have the answer?

The chat says it couldn't find it and can show your phone number or email instead. It does not guess.

What if I use up my answers?

The chat stops answering until the next month, or until you upgrade. You are never charged extra automatically.

We don't have documents ready. Can it read our website?

Yes. Type your website address in the dashboard and it reads the text of your pages. You can add files as well.

Can my staff use it too?

Yes. Invite each person by email from your dashboard and they can ask questions in the browser. Larger companies can use their own company login (single sign-on). Developers can also connect it to other apps: it speaks the same API format as OpenAI.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Your workspace moves back to the free plan and your documents are kept. See cancellation and refunds.
