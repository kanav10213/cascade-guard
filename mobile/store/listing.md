## Main store listing

**App name** (30 characters max)

**Short description** (80 characters max)

**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

Cascade Guard puts a chat on your business website. It answers visitors' questions from your own documents: your
fee list, timings, policies and brochures. When it can't answer, it asks the visitor for their phone number or
email and sends you an alert, so you can call back while they're still interested.

This app is the dashboard for business owners and their teams.

WHAT YOU CAN DO IN THE APP
• See every new lead, then call, WhatsApp, SMS or email them with one tap
• Get a phone alert the moment a visitor leaves their number
• Read every question visitors asked and the answer they were given
• See the questions your documents couldn't answer, so you know what to add
• Test the assistant with your own questions
• Manage your documents and your team

ANSWERS YOU CAN TRUST
Every answer is checked against your documents before a visitor sees it. Numbers, prices, dates and quotes must
match what your documents say. If they don't, the answer is corrected or the assistant says it doesn't know. It
never makes up a price.

MADE FOR SMALL BUSINESSES
Coaching classes, clinics, gyms, hostels, shops, travel agents, and any business that answers the same questions
every day.

GETTING STARTED
1. Sign up free in the app or at [your website].
2. Add your website address or upload your documents.
3. Paste one line of code into your website, or ask your web person to.
4. Turn on alerts. You're done.

The free plan includes 50 answers a month. Paid plans are managed on our website.

Privacy: we never sell your data or use your documents to train AI models. Policy: [your website]/privacy
